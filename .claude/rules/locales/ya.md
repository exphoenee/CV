# Yautja (ya) — Style Consistency Rules

Covers `scripts/locales/ya.js`. Yautja is the Predator species language from the Predator franchise.
Sparse, guttural, hunter-centric. Honor through the hunt. Economy of words.

---

## Core Character

- **Sparse, guttural, primal, honorable.** A Yautja says what is necessary — nothing more.
- Very short phrases. No redundancy. Hunt, observe, judge.
- Apostrophes indicate clicks or glottal stops — integral to the language's feel.
- `H` combinations (`Hk'`, `H'`) represent guttural sounds.

## Phonetic Character

- Hard consonants: `k`, `g`, `d`, `t`, `p`, `ch`
- Glottal clicks: `'` in positions like `Hk'nde`, `C'jit`, `H'dlak`, `di'tkae`
- `kw` clusters: `kwei` (warrior class), `kainde` (hunt/prey)
- Ending patterns: `-de`, `-ae`, `-nde`, `-tte`

## Style Rules

- Apostrophes and hyphens are part of the words — never remove: `Hk'nde`, `di'tkae`, `gkei'moun`, `nain-de`
- Very short phrases — if in doubt, make it shorter
- No politeness particles — Yautja does not soften statements
- Yautja speaks to equals or prey, not supplicants — tone is level, not subservient
- Tech proper names unchanged: `React`, `TypeScript`, `JSON`, `Swagger`
- `kwei` (capital `K` when used as a title: `Kwei`) denotes warrior rank/class

## Consistency Checks

- `Yin tekai:` for "CV of:" — the established opener
- `Aya Kwei na` or `Aya! Kwei ne!` for "hire me" — established phrases
- `Chiva yin` for Work Experience section
- `Del kehrite` for Education
- `Kainde lam` for Programming Languages
- `Hk'nde kwei` for Community & Mentorship
- `di'tkae` for "current" — not `sí` or `DaH` (those are other languages)
- `nau'gkon` for meeting/booking
- `pauk` for the "look/view" verb
- Apostrophes in `Hk'nde`, `di'tkae`, `gkei'moun`, `H'dlak`, `C'jit` — never omit
