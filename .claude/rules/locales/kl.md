# Klingon (kl) — Style Consistency Rules

Covers `scripts/locales/kl.js`. Inspired by tlhIngan Hol (Klingon) but adapted for a CV context.
Not strict linguistic Klingon — style and feel matter more than accuracy.

---

## Core Character

- **Aggressive, direct, honor-driven.** Every phrase implies strength or challenge.
- Short, commanding. No softening language.
- Object-Verb-Subject order in real Klingon, but labels use Subject-Verb-Object for readability.
- Apostrophes are integral to words — do not remove them.

## Phonetic Conventions

| Sound | Spelling | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Hard Q (uvular) | `Q` (capital) | `Qapla'`, `Qu'mey` |
| H (guttural) | `H` (capital) | `HoS`, `Hol`, `HaDlu'` |
| tlh sound | `tlh` | rare in labels |
| Glottal stop | `'` (apostrophe) | `jabbI'ID`, `ta'wI'`, `vIH` |
| Aspirated D | `D` (capital) | `DaH` (now), `De'` (data) |

**Capital letters are phonemic, not stylistic** — `Q`, `H`, `D`, `S`, `I` (capital i = different from lowercase l).

## Style Rules

- End exclamations with `!` — Klingon is emphatic: `"Qapla'!"`, `"HIvqa' veqlargh!"`
- Apostrophes in correct positions — never drop them: `"jabbI'ID"` not `"jabbID"`
- Capital letters are phonemic — `H`, `Q`, `D`, `S`, `I` in specific positions are mandatory
- Avoid mixing English grammar with Klingon vocabulary
- Humor is acceptable — Klingon CV is inherently comedic but internally consistent

## Consistency Checks

- `HIvqa' veqlargh!` for "hire me" — the established phrase, do not change
- `jabbI'ID` for message/transmission — apostrophes required
- `Qu'mey vIta'` for Work Experience — established section header
- `HaDlu' vIH` for Education — established
- `Hol ghItlh` for Programming Languages
- `DaH` for "current" — not `va'` or other alternatives
- `Qapla'` always ends success messages
- `yIngeH` for "send" (imperative form)
