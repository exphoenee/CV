# AI Specialist Sustainability — ismeretlen (SWICON partner)

> Language: English

## Required Qualifications

- Degree in Data Science, Computer Science or Sustainability Management with a focus on or proven skills in Data Science, Computer Science
- Experience with AI/ML technologies, ideally in a reporting or sustainability context
- Proficiency in data analysis tools (e.g. Power BI)
- At least 2-3 years of professional experience
- Good communication skills and experience in stakeholder engagement
- Excellent analytical skills, independent, structured, and solution-oriented working style
- Fluent in English
- Enthusiasm for sustainability

## Preferred Qualifications

- Good to deep understanding and knowledge of ESG frameworks and standards (e.g., CSRD, EU Taxonomy)
- Familiarity with ESG ratings and rankings (e.g. EcoVadis, CDP)
- German is a plus

## Responsibilities

- Help enhance and automate ESG (Environment, Social, Governance) reporting processes, particularly in line with the Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) including EU Taxonomy
- Contribute to improving ESG ratings and rankings (e.g. EcoVadis, CDP)
- Develop, program, test and implement AI-driven solutions
- Automate data flows and reporting processes
- Identify and integrate relevant ESG data sources
- Create dashboards and visualizations to monitor ESG performance
- Collaborate with internal and external stakeholders across Company (e.g., sustainability team, IT, data owners); particularly close cooperation with the Head of Sustainability at Company and the central sustainability team at the Hamburg headquarters
