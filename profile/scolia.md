Automatizált dart-scoring és analytics platform. **Link:** https://scoliadarts.com

Pioneering in the digitalisation of darts
Scolia is the first and and most popular darts autoscoring system.

Whether you are a keen hobby player or a professional – Scolia will make your practice smoother. With its advanced camera system and sophisticated algorithm, Scolia registers your throws with near perfect accuracy. No more interruptions from typing into an app, breaking your flow – Scolia announces your scores in real-time, shows you optional check-out suggestions and displays heatmaps of your game.

Track your progress and analyse your weak areas with our detailed statistics – thus skyrocket your darts skills in no time!

The Scolia application runs in the browser, which means seamless accessibility across devices. Use the Scolia app on any device – whether it's a smartphone, tablet, computer or TV.

The platform independent nature of the application offers additional benefits over native smartphone apps: it simplifies maintenance and updates, allowing us to roll out new features more quickly.

Primarily supported browsers are Google Chrome (latest version) and Safari (latest version).
