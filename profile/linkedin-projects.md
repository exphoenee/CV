## FACTS – Feedstock and Compliance Tracking System
**Oct 2023 – Present** · Associated with Aegex Technologies

FACTS is a cloud-based compliance and data management platform for companies that must meet EPA requirements (40 CFR §80.1454) when documenting biogenic feedstock origins. The FACTS Portal provides a secure, audit-ready environment to manage Source (point-of-origin) data and Bills of Lading (BOL/PTD), enabling confidential, role-based data exchange between buyers, sellers, and auditors. Built on Microsoft Azure with a scalable, serverless architecture.

## Hauni Engineering Projects (2010 – 2019)

### Automated Measurement Equipment for Resonance Detection
**Nov 2017 – May 2019** · Hauni Hungaria Gépgyártó Kft.  
PC + NI cDAQ chassis + Danfoss FC302 frequency converter system for automated frequency sweep measurements to detect resonance in vibratory conveyors. Communication via Ethernet (MCA121 extension card).

### Spring Stiffness Measuring and Labelling Device
**Aug 2017 – Feb 2019** · Hauni Hungaria Gépgyártó Kft.  
Machine for measuring and identifying spiral steel springs used for vibration isolation. Creates a database of results to assist workshop mechanics. Student thesis project (Zsolt Váci), led and supervised by Viktor.

### Automated Data Logging Unit
**Oct 2016 – Jan 2018** · Hauni Hungaria Gépgyártó Kft.  
Device for monitoring crank rod force to validate the lifetime and fatigue of rubber torsional springs, installed in a tobacco factory with remote online access. Later extended with a Danfoss FC302 for frequency sweep measurements.

### Spring Characteristic / Relaxation / Fatigue Test Equipment
**Nov 2017 – Jan 2018** · Hauni Hungaria Gépgyártó Kft.  
Two test machines for quasi-static and dynamic spring testing using strain gauge–based torque/force sensors and laser displacement sensors. LabVIEW automation by Zoltán; detailed mechanical design by Attila.

### Dynamic Deformation Measurement Algorithm in LabVIEW
**Oct 2016 – Jul 2017** · Hauni Hungaria Gépgyártó Kft.  
Piezoelectric accelerometer–based measurement producing slow-motion deformation animation. Used to countercheck stresses in thin sheet metal welded structures of vibratory conveyor troughs.

### Building Sheet-Metal Airflow Sensors
**Jan 2015 – May 2015** · Hauni Hungaria Gépgyártó Kft.  
Prandtl tubes from bonded sheet-metal parts with Motorola/Freescale MX2010DP differential pressure sensors. 8-sensor array measuring airflow across the cross-section of an optical tobacco sorter's ejection channel.

### Drive Torque Monitoring — Tobacco Silo
**Mar 2013 – Jun 2013** · Hauni Hungaria Gépgyártó Kft.  
Strain gauge–based system for simultaneous torque measurement on three tobacco-loosening rakes. Designed for worldwide deployment — kept as simple as possible. LabVIEW software.

### Detection of Resonance Problem on a Large Machine Platform
**Feb 2013 – Mar 2013** · Hauni Hungaria Gépgyártó Kft.  
6 uni-axial piezoelectric accelerometers in three directions at six locations on a 6×6×4 m machine frame. Recorded waterfall diagrams and frequency response functions in LabVIEW to diagnose a fan-induced resonance problem.

### Rubber Element Ageing Experiment
**Mar 2012 – Dec 2012** · Hauni Hungaria Gépgyártó Kft.  
Nearly 2,000-hour fatigue experiment measuring crank rod force and bending moment of a vibratory conveyor during rubber element ageing. Strain gauge sensor + LabVIEW application.

### New Product Development — 50 Hz Vibratory Conveyor
**Jul 2012 – Nov 2012** · Hauni Hungaria Gépgyártó Kft.  
Designed a 50 Hz vibratory conveyor for conveying foreign materials after an optical tobacco sorter. Amplitude: 0.4 mm, 12g peak acceleration.

### Drive Torque Measurement — Belt Conveyor
**Jun 2012 – Jul 2012** · Hauni Hungaria Gépgyártó Kft.  
Strain gauge–based instrument to measure the drive torque required for a cleaning brush on belt conveyors.

### Automated Data Acquisition System — Vibration Level Logging
**Jan 2010 – Mar 2012** · Hauni Hungaria Gépgyártó Kft.  
System to log vibration levels during operation to identify wear on plastic roller units in a chain conveyor. 72 piezoelectric accelerometers, 16 load cells, 8 encoders, 8 NI9188 cDAQ chassis, 8 PCs, Siemens PLC orchestration. 5,000+ hours of data.

### Dynamical Characteristics of Rubber Elements in Vibratory Conveyors
**Dec 2011 – Feb 2012** · Hauni Hungaria Gépgyártó Kft.  
Examined rubber component properties as a function of RPM and pretension using piezoelectric accelerometers and strain gauge sensors. LabVIEW measurement application.

### Measurement System for Quality Inspection
**Mar 2011 – May 2011** · Hauni Hungaria Gépgyártó Kft.  
Simple-to-use measurement system for checking crank rod force on vibratory conveyors during final quality inspection. Piezoelectric screw-on force sensor + LabVIEW data processing application.
